Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 28, 2026

Introduction

SmartVision FireGuard ("we", "our", or "the System") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered fire and smoke detection system.

Data We Collect

Account Information

We store your email address for account authentication, OTP verification, and emergency fire alerts.

Video Stream Data

Our AI processes video feeds in real-time to detect fire and smoke. Video files uploaded to the admin panel are stored locally on the server for processing.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide real-time AI fire and smoke detection monitoring.
  • To send critical emergency alerts via email when a fire is confirmed.
  • To verify account ownership through OTP (One-Time Password) mechanisms.
  • To manage and process surveillance video sources.

Data Security

We implement premium security standards to protect your data. All user passwords are encrypted using modern hashing algorithms. Video processing is localized within the application environment to ensure minimal data exposure.
